About InnovateED

Built by students, for students

InnovateED started as a single high school club project on invention rights. Today we're a nonprofit reaching classrooms across the country, teaching the fundamentals of patents, trademarks, and copyright before students ever file their first application.

Our story

We saw student inventors lose credit for their own ideas simply because no one taught them how IP works. So we built the curriculum we wished we'd had — approachable, practical, and free.

Since then we've run multiple workshops, mentored student inventors, and partnered with local professors and attorneys to bring more patent law and Intellectual Property education to the classroom.
